Trigeminal neuralgia is considered one of the most painful conditions. Its symptoms are so intense that they can turn a patient's life upside down, stripping away comfort and the ability to perform ordinary activities.

## Why Standard Treatment Does Not Always Suffice

First-line treatment includes anticonvulsants (e.g., gabapentin, pregabalin), antidepressants, and — in more severe cases — opioids. Sometimes nerve blocks are also used.

Unfortunately:

- in some patients, these approaches work poorly,
- in others, first-line medications cause side effects (drowsiness, dizziness, concentration problems).

It is therefore worth looking at medical cannabis, which may serve as a complement or alternative to existing treatment methods.

## How Medical Cannabis Helps Neuropathic Pain

Cannabis contains active compounds called cannabinoids:

- **THC** — responsible for psychoactive effects, but also strongly analgesic,
- **CBD** — not psychoactive, with additional anti-inflammatory and neuroprotective properties.

These compounds interact with the endocannabinoid system — the body's natural system for regulating pain and tension. As a result, they:

- reduce the intensity of neuropathic pain,
- improve sleep quality,
- reduce stress and tension associated with chronic symptoms,
- support overall quality of life for people living with neuralgia.

## What Does the Research Say?

As early as 2008 and 2010, studies showed that patients using cannabis several times daily reported reduced pain, improved sleep, and better mood.

In a group of oncology patients with neuropathic pain, 43% experienced a pain reduction of approximately 30%, particularly when using preparations combining THC and CBD.

Importantly, in many cases patients were able to reduce their opioid doses — medications that are highly addictive and burdensome on the body.

## Why This Matters for Trigeminal Neuralgia

In trigeminal neuralgia, standard treatment does not always provide the expected relief. Medical cannabis may:

- reduce the severity of pain attacks,
- improve tolerance of daily activities (eating, speaking, hygiene),
- allow reduction of other medications that burden the body.

## Is Cannabis a Perfect Solution?

No — but it can be a valuable component of therapy.

Possible side effects (e.g., dry mouth, drowsiness, dizziness) are usually mild and transient. The best results are achieved with well-selected THC-to-CBD ratios, which requires physician supervision.
