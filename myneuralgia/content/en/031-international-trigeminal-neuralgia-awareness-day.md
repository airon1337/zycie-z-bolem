On October 7, 2025, we mark the eighth International Trigeminal Neuralgia Awareness Day, a day dedicated to speaking about a condition that remains invisible in everyday life yet profoundly affects those who live with it.

As in previous years, the goal of this campaign is to increase awareness and understanding for people facing this extraordinarily challenging type of pain.

Trigeminal neuralgia is a condition characterized by sudden, piercing attacks of facial pain, often compared to an electric shock.

However, this disease also has another, less well-known face: chronic, constant, burning, sometimes throbbing pain that accompanies patients for hours, days, or even weeks. Although its intensity may vary, it is present nearly all the time, leading to profound fatigue and a sense of isolation.

Yet this day is also a symbol of strength, courage, and perseverance.

Everyone living with trigeminal neuralgia or painful trigeminal neuropathy demonstrates daily how much a person can endure and how deep human resilience can run.

The TN patient community is growing. More and more people share their experiences, support one another, and offer hope to those who are only just receiving their diagnosis.

Today, on this special day, I want to say one thing:

You are not alone.

Although pain can dominate your thoughts and your days, there are moments of relief, there are people who understand, and there is hope, for better treatment, greater public awareness, and a more peaceful life. Every step, even the smallest, toward understanding this disease matters.

May this eighth edition of Awareness Day remind everyone that courage is the everyday reality of people with trigeminal neuralgia, and that every bit of support, every word of comfort, and every gesture of empathy can be a ray of light in their darkest moments.
