Hi, my name is Natalia, and since 2014 I have been been living with chronic pain caused by trigeminal nerve damage.

My story began suddenly. Almost overnight, I developed severe pain in my jaw. I thought it was temporary, but it turned out to be the beginning of a long journey of surgeries, specialist appointments, trying different therapies, and learning to live with pain that has stayed with me ever since.

For more than 11 years, I have been living with neuropathy, pain that never truly goes away. I have learned that every day brings a new challenge, but also a new opportunity to find strength within myself.

Why am I creating this website?

Because I know that chronic pain changes lives. I want to create a place for people who live with it too, and for the people who love and support them.

I will share my story, my everyday life, and the latest developments from medicine and scientific research.

I want this website to offer hope, support, and the reassurance that none of us has to face this journey alone.

If chronic pain is part of your life, I invite you to join me here. Together, we can find a little light even on the most difficult days.
