Autologous Blood Therapy for Temporomandibular Joint Pain

Author: Natalia · Date: 2026-01-28 · 1 min read
Category: Treatment & Procedures

At the Wrocław University Dental Center in Poland, patients suffering from temporomandibular joint (TMJ) pain can now access modern therapies utilizing preparations derived from their own blood. These methods belong to a group of autologous regenerative therapies aimed at relieving pain and improving the function of the joints, as well as the muscles of the head and neck.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is trigeminal neuralgia?

Trigeminal neuralgia is a chronic facial pain condition. It manifests as sudden, severe, usually one-sided pain attacks — often described as an electric shock. A single attack typically lasts from a few seconds to about two minutes.

Which doctor should I see for facial pain?

First, see a neurologist. If surgery is being considered, a neurosurgeon. If a dental cause is suspected, it is also worth ruling it out with a dentist.
