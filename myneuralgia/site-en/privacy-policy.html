Privacy Policy

Who we are

This website (myneuralgia.com) is a personal blog run by Natalia, providing educational content about trigeminal neuralgia and chronic pain.

What data we collect

  • Email address — only if you voluntarily subscribe to our newsletter
  • Analytics data — anonymized page views and site usage through privacy-respecting analytics
  • Cookies — minimal, functional cookies required for the site to work properly

How we use your data

  • Newsletter emails — to send you occasional content about living with trigeminal neuralgia
  • Analytics — to understand which content is helpful and improve the site

Third-party services

  • MailerLite — email newsletter service (processes email addresses of subscribers)
  • Cloudflare — hosting and CDN (may process IP addresses for security)

Your rights

You can:

  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time (one-click link in every email)
  • Request deletion of your data by contacting us through Facebook
  • Request a copy of your personal data

Data retention

Newsletter subscriber data is kept until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is anonymized and aggregated.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us through our Facebook page.
