Hi, My Name Is Natalia — Living with Chronic Pain Since 2014

Author: Natalia · Date: 2025-09-17 · 2 min read
Category: Living with Pain

Hi, my name is Natalia, and since 2014 I have been living with chronic pain caused by trigeminal nerve damage.

My story began suddenly — overnight, severe pain appeared in my jaw. I thought it would pass, but it turned out to be the beginning of a long journey of surgeries, specialist consultations, trying different therapies, and learning to live with pain that never went away.

For over 11 years now, neuropathy has been my constant companion — a pain that never truly disappears. I have learned that every day brings a new challenge, but also a new opportunity to find strength within myself.

Why I Created This Website

Because I know that chronic pain changes everything. I want to create a space for those who live with it too — and for the people who care about them.

I will share my story, my everyday life, and the latest developments from medicine and scientific research.

If chronic pain is part of your life — I invite you to be here with me. Together, we can find a little light even on the most difficult days.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is trigeminal neuralgia?

Trigeminal neuralgia is a chronic facial pain condition. It manifests as sudden, severe, usually one-sided pain attacks — often described as an electric shock. A single attack typically lasts from a few seconds to about two minutes.

What is the difference between neuralgia and neuropathy?

In simple terms: neuralgia primarily involves paroxysmal pain along a nerve, usually without loss of sensation. Neuropathy involves nerve damage that more often causes constant pain along with numbness and sensory loss.
