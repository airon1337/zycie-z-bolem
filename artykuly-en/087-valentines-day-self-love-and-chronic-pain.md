Today, on Valentine's Day, I want to share something more than traditional wishes.

I want to share a sound and an intention — directed toward those who face trigeminal nerve pain, neuralgia, or neuropathy every single day.

In the photo, I'm striking a gong — a symbol of cleansing, harmony, and positive energy. May its vibration reach you too. May it bring even a moment of comfort and serve as a reminder that the most important love always begins within ourselves.

In a world that so often celebrates outward love — romantic, spectacular, full of gestures and symbols — you know that there is also a deeper dimension. It's a love born in silence. In patience. In a breath full of tenderness toward your own body. It's a love that doesn't need a stage or an audience — awareness and presence are enough.

May this day remind you that you deserve everything good. Rest. Peace. Words of support that don't need to be loud to carry real power.

Your pain does not define who you are. You are sensitive, strong, and remarkably aware people. Every day, you learn to live in harmony with what is difficult. That is a quiet act of courage that often remains invisible to the world — and yet it is a true form of heroism.

May self-love become your daily refuge. May it be gentle like the touch of morning sun.

Patient like the wind that always returns.

Constant like the rhythm of breath.

Remember — kindness toward yourself is not weakness. It is strength. It is a way to survive, to reclaim peace and joy despite everything.

And gratitude — even for the smallest moments: a ray of light coming through the window, the scent of tea, someone's warm smile — may it give you strength when it feels like there's none left.

Today, may the heart of each of you hear one simple message:

You are enough.

You matter.

You are loved — including by yourself.

And with the sound of the gong ringing in the photo, may a wave of peace, light, and good energy flow toward you. May each of its echoes remind you that even in silence, even in pain — you still resonate with Love.
